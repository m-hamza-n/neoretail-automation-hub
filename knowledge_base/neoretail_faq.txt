NeoRetail Customer Service Knowledge Base:

RETURNS POLICY:
- Customers have 30 days from delivery to return items.
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, with original tags attached.
- To initiate a return, customers email returns@neoretail.com with their order ID.
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the item.
- Sale items are final sale and cannot be returned.

SHIPPING POLICY:
- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days.
- Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs an additional $15.
- Free standard shipping on orders over $150.
- International shipping is available to 30+ countries, takes 7-14 business days.
- Tracking information is emailed once the order ships.

SIZING:
- NeoRetail sizes run true to size.
- Size guide is available on each product page.
- If between sizes, we recommend sizing up for oversized fits and sizing down for fitted styles.
- Exchanges for size issues are free within 30 days.

PAYMENT:
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay.
- Payment is charged at time of order placement.
- We do not store card details.

CONTACT:
- Customer support: support@neoretail.com
- Support hours: Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm EST.
- Response time: within 24 hours on business days.
